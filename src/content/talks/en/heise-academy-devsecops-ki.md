description: >
  Classroom course at heise Academy, co-delivered with Andreas Falk, across four sessions (November 6, 13, 20, and 27, 2025): discovering the synergies between DevSecOps and AI, integrating AI-assisted security into the DevOps pipeline, building a secure value-stream supply chain across DevOps/CI/CD/observability, and IT security with AI from prevention to reporting. The course shows how DevSecOps practices and AI tooling complement each other — and where AI introduces new security risks: blind spots in classical SAST tools for AI-generated code, hallucinated package names as supply-chain attack surfaces, prompt injection in CI/CD pipelines. Actionable guidance for teams already using AI coding assistants in production.
